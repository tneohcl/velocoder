VeloCoder can use different parts of your computer to convert video.

**Automatic** is recommended. VeloCoder chooses an available processing method for you.

**CPU** uses your computer's main processor.

Compatible graphics hardware may also appear, such as **Intel** or **AMD**. Hardware processing can convert video faster on supported systems.

The choices shown depend on the hardware available on your computer. If no compatible graphics encoder is available, VeloCoder uses the CPU.
