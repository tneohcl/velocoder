**Choose the output's frame size.**

![The Resolution field on the Video tab](video-settings.png)

**Keep Original** leaves the frame size untouched -- the right choice unless you have a specific reason to change it.

Picking a smaller resolution (e.g. 1080p from a 4K source) shrinks the file further on top of whatever Quality setting you've chosen, at the cost of detail. VeloCoder never upscales past the source's own resolution.
