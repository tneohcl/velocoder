The label above **Encoding** tells you exactly what the settings panel is currently editing:

- **Settings for new videos** -- these will apply to the next video you add.
- **Settings for "filename"** / **Settings for N selected videos** -- you've selected one or more videos already in the queue, and any change here edits their settings directly instead.

Nothing you do to an already-queued video's settings changes the defaults used for videos you add afterward, and vice versa.
