VeloCoder converts video files to a different codec, quality, or size -- add one or more videos, adjust the settings on the left if you want, and press Convert.

Most people never need to touch anything beyond the Video and Audio tabs. Everything there has a plain-language explanation; nothing requires knowing how video encoding actually works under the hood.

If you want more control, the **Expert** section (bottom of the Video tab) exposes the exact numbers behind Quality and Speed. It's optional -- Normal's defaults are a reasonable choice for most videos.
