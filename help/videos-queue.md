The queue on the right lists every video waiting to convert, in the order they'll run. Drag a row to reorder it, or select one or more rows and use **Remove** in the toolbar (or the Delete key, or **Edit → Remove Selected**) to take them out of the queue.

Each row shows the source video's own properties (resolution, duration, size) -- the settings it will actually convert *with* live in the panel on the left, whenever that row is selected.
