**Convert video files to a different codec, quality, or size.**

Add one or more videos, adjust the settings on the left if you want, and press Convert.

![VeloCoder's main window, with the queue on the right and settings on the left](quick-start.png "① Add Videos ② Choose settings ③ Save to ④ Convert")

1. **Add Videos** -- drag files into the queue, or click the button.
2. **Choose settings** -- Quality, Format, and the rest, on the left.
3. **Save to** -- where the converted files are written.
4. **Convert** -- starts the queue.

Most people never need to touch anything beyond the Video and Audio tabs. Everything there has a plain-language explanation; nothing requires knowing how video encoding actually works under the hood.

> **Tip**
> The **Expert** section (bottom of the Video tab) exposes the exact numbers behind Quality and Speed. It's optional -- Normal's defaults are a reasonable choice for most videos.
