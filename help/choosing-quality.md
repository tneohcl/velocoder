The Quality row gives you three plain choices:

- **Smaller File** -- noticeably smaller, with some visible quality loss on close inspection.
- **Balanced** -- a sensible default for most videos.
- **Better Quality** -- closer to the original, at a larger file size.

These three map to different exact numbers depending on which hardware is actually doing the work -- Processing above (Automatic/CPU/Intel/AMD) picks that, and you never need to know the underlying number unless you open Expert.
