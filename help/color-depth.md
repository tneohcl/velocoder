**Choose the output's color depth.**

![The Color Depth field on the Video tab](video-settings.png)

**10-bit** stores finer color detail, giving smoother gradients (skies, shadows) with less visible banding. It may be less compatible with older devices or software.

**8-bit** is the traditional standard and has the widest possible compatibility.

> **Note**
> If you chose H.264 specifically for compatibility, consider 8-bit too -- 10-bit H.264 gives up some of that same compatibility again.
