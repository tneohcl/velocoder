**Add a video, choose settings, and press Convert.**

![VeloCoder's main window, with the queue on the right and settings on the left](quick-start.png "① Add Videos ② Choose settings ③ Save to ④ Convert")

1. Add a video by dragging it into the queue on the right, or clicking **Add Videos...**.
2. Adjust settings on the left if the defaults don't fit -- these apply to every video you add next, and to whichever video is selected in the queue.
3. Press **Convert**.

The converted file is written to the folder shown next to **Save to:** at the bottom of the queue, without touching or replacing your original.
