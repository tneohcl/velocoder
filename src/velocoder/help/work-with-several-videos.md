Add as many videos as you like -- they convert one at a time, in the order they appear in the queue. Drag a row up or down to reorder it.

Select one video in the queue to give it its own settings, different from the rest. Select several at once to change a setting on all of them together (each keeps whatever else was already different between them).

Press **Convert** once and VeloCoder works through the whole queue on its own.
