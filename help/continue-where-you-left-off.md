Closing VeloCoder doesn't lose your queue. The next time you open it, anything that hadn't finished converting -- still waiting, interrupted mid-convert, or failed -- comes back exactly as it was, with its own settings and in the same order, along with the Save-to folder you had chosen.

Videos that had already finished converting don't come back -- there's nothing left to do with them. Anything VeloCoder can't find anymore (moved or deleted since your last session) is quietly left out, with a one-line note if that happened.

An interrupted video restarts from the beginning rather than resuming partway through.
